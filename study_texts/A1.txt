When you are a child, one year feels very long. The summer holiday feels like it will never end. You wait for your birthday, and it seems very far away. But when you are an adult, time moves fast. A year goes by quickly. Then suddenly it is winter again. Why does this happen? Why does time feel faster when we get older?

There are a few ideas. Here is the first one. When you are five years old, one year is a big part of your life. It is one part in five. But when you are fifty, one year is a very small part of your life. It is one part in fifty. So each new year feels smaller than the year before. The same amount of time feels shorter and shorter. A year is still a year on the clock. But it does not feel the same.

Here is a second idea. It is about memory. When something is new, your brain makes a strong memory. It keeps many small details. When you look back later, that time feels full and long. But when you do the same thing every day, your brain makes fewer memories. There is nothing new to keep. So that time feels thin and short when you look back.

Think about a child. A child does many things for the first time. The first day at school. The first time in the sea. The first bike ride. Every day brings something new. Now think about an adult. An adult often does the same things again and again. The same road to work. The same food. The same week. There are not many new things, so there are not many new memories. So the years start to feel the same.

Attention is also important. When you are bored and you watch the clock, time feels slow. When you are busy and happy, time feels fast. So clock time and felt time are not the same. The clock does not change, but your feeling of time can change.

It is important to say one thing. Scientists do not fully agree about this. They have ideas, but they are not sure which one is right. Maybe all of them are a little bit true. There is still a lot that we do not know.

So what can we do? Maybe we can try new things more often. We can go to new places. We can learn something new. We can meet new people. We can look more carefully at small things each day. This will not stop the clock. But it may help our days feel full again. And it may help us remember more of our life.
